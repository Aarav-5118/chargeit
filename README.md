# ChargeIt

Book a neighbour's home EV charger in Ahmedabad, or list your own and earn from its idle hours.

This is a working prototype built with vibe coding. Everything runs in one file, `index.html`.

## What it does
- Picks the best charger for your car and battery level, then books it with one swipe
- Matches chargers to your vehicle (cars, scooters and autos) and shows real charging time
- Rebook your last charger, live charging screen, battery rescue (SOS), highway stops, cable rental
- Hosts can list a charger, set hours and prices, and see what they could earn
- Works on phones and on laptops (full website layout on wide screens)

## Good to know
- Hosts, prices, ratings and the rescue van are sample data
- Bookings and listings are saved only in each visitor's own browser for now
- Vehicle battery sizes and AC charging limits are carmaker figures
